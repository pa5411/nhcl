### How to print

> [!TIP]
> We can provide assistance at any point if needed.

{{< steps >}}
{{< step number="1" title="Log into the computer" >}}Pick a computer and sign in{{< /step >}}
{{< step number="2" title="Open the file" >}}Open the file you wish to print{{< /step >}}
{{< step number="3" title="Send to printer" >}}Press the print button{{< /step >}}
{{< step number="4" title="Check the settings" >}}Select the Samsung printer, make it colour if desired{{< /step >}}
{{< step number="5" title="Talk to a volunteer" >}}A volunteer will print your document at the printer{{< /step >}}
{{< /steps >}}

### Printer FAQs

{{< accordion mode="collapse" >}}
  {{< accordionItem title="Can I print something directly from my phone or laptop?" >}}
  We do not offer WiFi printing. 
  
  However, if you are unable to log into one of our computers to access your documents, you can send your document(s) from your device to our secure email address. We can then print the documents and will delete the email immediately afterwards.  
  {{< /accordionItem >}}

  {{< accordionItem title="can I print something directly from a USB stick?" >}}
  Yes, all of our computers have USB Type A and USB Type C ports.
  {{< /accordionItem >}}
{{< /accordion >}}
