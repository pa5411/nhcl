## Overview 

> [!TIP]
> Our space can also be hired outside our regular opening hours. Please email northharrowlibrary@gmail.com for further enquiries and pricing.

The Library is available to hire for space to host a wide variety of events. 

## Multi-Use Space

The Library has a significant communal seating area which is regularly used for activities such as children's arts and crafts and knitting. We can also move the shelving units and tables to provide additional space for activities requiring an open space, including yoga, dancing classes and robotics lessons. 

## Back Room 

An air-conditioned room, offering a quiet and private space for meetings and activities, away from the hustle and bustle of the rest of the Library.
